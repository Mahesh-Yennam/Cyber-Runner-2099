⚡ Cyber Protocol & Power-Ups

⬆️
Jump & Double Jump: Press Spacebar or W to leap over floor spikes. Tap again in mid-air to double jump!
⬇️
Slide Duck: Press Arrow Down or S to slide under overhead laser barriers and drones.
💎
Data Chits: Glowing cyan and gold diamonds add credit multipliers to your final score (+10 Pts).
🛡️
Cyber Shield: Grants a 1-hit kinetic bubble absorbing any obstacle collision.
🧲
Data Magnet: Pulls all nearby data chits toward your runner for 7 seconds.
⚡
Overdrive Sprint: Accelerates to maximum velocity and obliterates all obstacles for 4 seconds.

Desktop Controls: Spacebar / Arrow Up to Jump (tap twice for Double Jump). Arrow Down / S to Slide. P to Pause.
Mobile Controls: Tap the large ⬆ JUMP and ⬇ SLIDE buttons, or swipe up and down directly across the screen.
